Our Kanto Fried Chicken

This business is based on a small cart at the corner of the street, Kanto Fried Chicken was born out of the great
chefs that uses a good quality Chicken in order to give filipinos the "Lutong Pinoy" flavors to everyone.

Why Kanto Fried Chicken is the best?

Kanto Fried Chicken stands out because it delivers the perfect combination of flavor, quality, and comfort in every bite.
What makes it special is its juicy and tender chicken, marinated with rich seasonings that go deep into the meat,
not just on the surface. Every piece is cooked to perfection with a crispy golden coating that gives a satisfying crunch while keeping the inside moist and flavorful.
